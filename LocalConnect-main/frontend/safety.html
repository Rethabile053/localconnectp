Safety Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority. Follow these guidelines to ensure safe and successful transactions.

🏪

Meet in Public Places

Always meet in well-lit, public locations like coffee shops, grocery store parking lots, or police station safe exchange zones. Never invite strangers to your home or go to theirs.

👥

Bring a Friend

Whenever possible, bring a friend or family member with you to the meeting. There's safety in numbers, and an extra set of eyes can help verify the item.

💵

Use Cash or Secure Payment

Cash is king for local transactions. If using digital payment, verify the transfer is complete before handing over the item. Never accept checks from strangers or wire money.

📱

Keep Communication In-App

Use LocalConnect's messaging system for all communication. This creates a record and protects your personal contact information until you're comfortable sharing it.

🔍

Inspect Before You Buy

Thoroughly inspect the item before completing the transaction. Test electronics, check for damage, and verify it matches the listing description. Don't feel pressured to buy if something feels off.

🚩

Trust Your Instincts

If a deal seems too good to be true, it probably is. If a seller is pushy, refuses to meet in public, or asks for unusual payment methods, walk away. Report suspicious behavior to us immediately.

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or feel unsafe, contact our support team immediately.
